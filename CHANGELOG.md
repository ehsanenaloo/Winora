## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-02

### Added
- Desktop app for Windows 11 with English and Persian, light and dark themes, and a right-to-left layout.
- Customize, Optimize and Privacy pages for File Explorer, the taskbar, Start, performance and privacy settings, each with a risk level.
- A review before every change, a History page with undo, and an Action center for waiting changes and running tasks.
- Profiles: Balanced, Speed, Security, Privacy, Gaming and Minimal.
- Power, Network and Windows Update pages.
- Startup, Scheduled tasks and Services pages to turn items on or off.
- Apps and Preinstalled apps pages to remove programs and Store apps, with a Quarantine for leftover folders.
- Cleanup with Free up space and Disk usage.
- Repair tools and optional Windows features.
- A first-start guide with a restore point and a profile.
- Search across settings, pages and tools (Ctrl+K).
- A command-line tool, `winora`.
